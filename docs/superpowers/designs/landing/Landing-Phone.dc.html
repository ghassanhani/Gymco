FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need an account?

No. Everything works on your phone without one. An account only adds a backup and the daily photo-scan counter.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Search needs a connection, but Write works offline in all three languages and every log syncs to your diary straight away.

What does the AI see?

Only the photo or text you choose to scan. It goes to Anthropic for the estimate and is not kept. Every AI row is tagged, and you confirm it before it counts.

Does it read Apple Health or Health Connect?

If you allow it: weight and steps in, workouts and nutrition out. Each switch is separate, and turning one off never blocks the app.

Is Arabic fully right-to-left?

Yes. Layout, arrows, swipes and the week strip all mirror, set in IBM Plex Sans Arabic.

What will Pro cost?

€4.99 a month, €39.99 a year, or €89 once. Nothing is for sale during testing, and every Pro feature is unlocked.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app to remove everything on your phone. With an account, delete it from Settings and the backup goes within 24 hours.
